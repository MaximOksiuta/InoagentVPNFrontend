# Inoagent: свой маршрут

Applied skill: anthropics/skills/frontend-design, installed 2026-11-08.

Palette: graphite #141415, panel #1d1d20, chalk #f0ede7, muted #a9a5a0,
copper #e5aa86, working connection #71bdb0. Copper marks actions and the
route identity; green and red retain their actual status meanings.
Type: IBM Plex Sans / Segoe UI, one family, 40/24/16/13 scale. Left aligned.

A large hero on every screen would consume space needed by the catalog.
The route drawing is large at sign-in, compact beside the page heading,
and the catalog remains a working table.

    [route mark + name]    [devices / configs / servers]    [account]
    [page title + explanation]                     [route drawing]
    [real availability, notifications and queued work]
    [server rail] [filters / batch action / configurations table]

The mark consists of two paths around a shared junction; illustrations repeat
the same bend radius and passage. No shields, stock art or decorative numbers.
Initial route animation runs once; hover/focus retraces the route. Reduced
motion disables movement. Illustration makes no availability claim. Accent
moved from a generic neon network palette to copper on the user-approved
graphite background. Data areas remain neutral for readability.
